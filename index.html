AI steel takeoff from structural drawings

Estimators count every column, beam, truss and brace in a structural drawing set by hand before they can price a steel job. This system reads the whole PDF and returns the counts and a labour-hour estimate. The plan scanner at the top of this page shows the idea.

~90%less effort per drawing set: about 10 minutes instead of 1.6 hours, across 35 sets a day
  • Qwen3-VL
  • YOLO
  • PyMuPDF
  • Shapely
  • Detectron2
  • Docling
  • AWS Step Functions
  • Lambda
  • Bedrock
How it works
Problem
Seven estimators spent their days counting steel on 100+ page drawing sets. Slow, and easy to miss a piece.
Approach
A VLM sorts the pages, tiled YOLO finds symbols, and vector geometry reads beams, columns and grid lines straight from the PDF. Dedicated readers handle trusses, bracing, knee braces and L-angles.
Result
Validated on 60+ real drawing sets. A reduce stage made 4.3× faster (302 s to 70 s), and every release is gated on zero regressions across all of them.
01
Sort the pagesVLM labels plans, sections, schedules and details
↓ parallel workers, one per page batch
02
Find the symbolsTiled YOLO at 200 DPI; grid bubbles from vector circles
03
Read the geometryBeam and column lines, truss spans at the sheet's own scale
04
De-duplicate across sheetsGrid-anchored matching so a column drawn twice counts once
05
Count and estimateComponent totals and labour hours for the estimator

Kubera Finance

A personal project you can use right now: bank statements in, categorised spending out.

Problem
Every German bank exports statements in its own format, and sorting a year of bookings into categories by hand takes evenings.
Built
A pipeline that reads PDF and Excel statements from four banks, removes duplicates from overlapping statements, sets aside transfers between my own accounts and spots recurring contracts. Well-known shops are sorted by rule; everything else goes to Gemini, checked against a fixed list of 61 categories.
Result
A monthly dashboard of income, spending and contracts, and a public demo where anyone can upload a statement and watch each booking get its category.
4 banksDKB, N26, Deutsche Bank and Barclays, read automatically
