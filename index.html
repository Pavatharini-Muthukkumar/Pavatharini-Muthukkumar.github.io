2026 · Technical leadFlagship

Automated steel takeoff from structural drawings

Before quoting a steel structure, estimators count every column, beam, truss and brace manually in drawing sets of 100+ pages. The system processes the complete PDF and returns component quantities and a labour-hour estimate.

~90%less effort per drawing set: approx. 10 minutes instead of 1.6 hours, at 35 drawing sets per day
  • Qwen3.6
  • Qwen3-VL
  • Agentic workflow
  • YOLO
  • PyMuPDF
  • Shapely
  • Detectron2
  • Docling
  • AWS Step Functions
  • Lambda
  • Bedrock
How it works
Problem
Seven estimators performed the takeoff manually: time-consuming and prone to omissions.
Approach
A vision-language model classifies the pages, tiled YOLO detects symbols, and vector geometry extracts beams, columns and grid lines directly from the PDF. Trusses and horizontal and vertical bracing are counted with geometric rules. L-angles, knee braces, embed angles, loose plates and bent plates are handled by an agentic workflow: each section is sent to Qwen3.6 together with a component card that describes how the component appears in a section; geometric rules then validate the count, and every mismatch is returned to Qwen3.6 for re-validation.
Result
Validated on 60+ real drawing sets. Reduce stage accelerated 4.3× (302 s → 70 s); every release requires zero regressions across all validated sets.

Professional project · 2026

Process a drawing set

  1. Page classification: a vision-language model distinguishes plans, schedules, sections and general notes.
  2. Component recognition: geometric rules count beams, columns, trusses and bracing; each section is sent to Qwen3.6 with a component card (L-angle, knee brace, embed angle, loose or bent plate) describing how to identify it.
  3. Validation: geometric rules check every count, mismatches are returned to Qwen3.6 for re-validation, and components shown on several sheets are counted once.
